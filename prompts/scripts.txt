Generate a high-retention Islamic YouTube Shorts script optimised for AI voiceover.

------------------------------------------------------------
LAYER 1 — CORE RULES
------------------------------------------------------------

- Script must be under 25 seconds read time
- Use simple, natural spoken language (NOT formal writing)
- No fake hadiths or Quran quotes
- No fabricated Islamic rulings
- If unsure, reframe as reflection or question

Tone:
- Serious
- Direct
- Emotionally engaging
- Personal (speaking TO the viewer, not about them)

------------------------------------------------------------
LAYER 2 — VOICE DELIVERY SYSTEM (CRITICAL)
------------------------------------------------------------

Write for AI voiceover performance:

- Short sentences (max 6–10 words preferred)
- Natural pauses using line breaks
- No long or complex sentences
- No robotic phrasing
- No filler words
- Each line must carry emotional weight

Every sentence must:
- push tension forward
- sound natural when spoken aloud
- feel like real human speech

------------------------------------------------------------
LAYER 3 — EMOTIONAL ENGINE
------------------------------------------------------------

Script must strongly use at least one:
- Guilt (you know this… but still ignore it)
- Fear (what if this costs you…)
- Doubt (are you actually doing this right?)
- Urgency (you don’t have as much time as you think)
- Reflection (you’ve never thought about this properly)

Emotion must escalate throughout.

------------------------------------------------------------
LAYER 4 — STRUCTURE (MANDATORY)

1. HOOK (1–2 lines)
   - Immediate tension
   - Direct and confronting
   - Must create curiosity gap instantly

2. BUILD-UP
   - Clear, simple explanation
   - Emotional escalation
   - No fluff or wasted words

3. FINAL LINE
   - Strong emotional hit OR reflective question
   - If question feels unnatural base purely on trying to bring a strong emotion(Do not finish by increasing guilt)
   - Do not end in pure Guilt and cause viewer frustration
   - Must feel unresolved (forces engagement)

4. LOOP SYSTEM
   - Ending must connect seamlessly back to the hook
   - Should feel like a continuous cycle when replayed

------------------------------------------------------------
LAYER 5 — RETENTION OPTIMISATION

- First 2 seconds must create tension
- No slow build-ups
- Remove anything that does not increase curiosity or emotion
- Keep flow tight and continuous
- Every line must justify its existence

------------------------------------------------------------
LAYER 6 — QUALITY FILTER

Reject and rewrite internally if:
- sounds robotic
- too generic
- lacks emotional tension
- does not flow naturally when spoken
- loop is weak or forced

Only output strong, emotionally engaging scripts.

------------------------------------------------------------
FINAL RULE

Script must sound powerful when read aloud by AI.
If it does not sound natural, rewrite it.
