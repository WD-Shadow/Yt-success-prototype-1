Generate HIGH-CTR YouTube Shorts titles.

------------------------------------------------------------
CORE OBJECTIVE
------------------------------------------------------------

Titles must:
- create immediate curiosity
- feel slightly uncomfortable
- hint at a hidden truth
-make the youtube algorithm push it to audience

------------------------------------------------------------
HARD CONSTRAINTS
------------------------------------------------------------

- Max 9 words
- Must create tension in first 3 words
- Must NOT be generic or overused
- Must not be false

Avoid:
- “Is your ___ accepted?”
- “Nobody told you this”
- “What if…”

------------------------------------------------------------
STYLE
------------------------------------------------------------

Use:
- contradiction
- exposure
- internal conflict

Examples of direction:
- “You already gave up on this…”
- “You’re doing this without noticing”
- “This is why nothing feels right”
- "You must do x"

------------------------------------------------------------
CLICK MECHANISM
------------------------------------------------------------

Each title must:
- make viewer feel slightly exposed
- suggest something is wrong
- create need to resolve tension
- Force mental need to immediately look at it

------------------------------------------------------------
SAFETY RULES
------------------------------------------------------------

- No fake religious claims
- No fabricated rulings

------------------------------------------------------------
FINAL FILTER
------------------------------------------------------------

If it feels like a common YouTube title → rewrite.

Only output sharp, curiosity-driven titles.

Give high CTR title.
