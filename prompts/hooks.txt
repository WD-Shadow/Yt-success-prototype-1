Generate 5 HIGH-CTR Islamic YouTube Shorts hooks.

------------------------------------------------------------
CORE OBJECTIVE
------------------------------------------------------------
Each hook must feel:
- uncomfortable
- personal
- pattern-breaking
- impossible to ignore

If it feels familiar or predictable → REJECT and rewrite.

------------------------------------------------------------
HARD CONSTRAINTS (MANDATORY)
------------------------------------------------------------

Each hook MUST:
- create tension in the FIRST 5 words
- target a specific internal thought, mistake, or behaviour
- include at least ONE:
  - contradiction (you think X… but actually Y)
  - exposure (you’re doing this… without realising)
  - internal conflict (you want X… but your actions show Y)

Each hook MUST NOT:
- sound like common Islamic reminders
- use overused phrases:
  - “your dua”
  - “is it accepted”
  - “are you doing this”
  - “what if this is your last…”
- be vague or broad
- feel like something seen before

------------------------------------------------------------
PATTERN BREAK ENGINE
------------------------------------------------------------

Hooks should feel like:
- calling out a hidden flaw
- revealing something uncomfortable
- exposing a silent habit
- flipping a belief the viewer assumes is correct

------------------------------------------------------------
STYLE
------------------------------------------------------------

- 1 line only (max 12 words)
- sharp, direct, confrontational
- no soft tone
- no explanations
- no filler

------------------------------------------------------------
EXAMPLES OF DIRECTION (NOT TO COPY)
------------------------------------------------------------

“You stopped expecting your dua to work.”

“You’re praying… but your mind checked out already.”

“You don’t actually want Allah to answer this.”

------------------------------------------------------------
SAFETY RULES
------------------------------------------------------------

- Do NOT invent Quran or hadith
- Do NOT make religious claims without certainty
- Frame as reflection, not absolute statements if unsure

------------------------------------------------------------
FINAL FILTER
------------------------------------------------------------

Before output:
- If hook feels generic → rewrite
- If hook feels safe → rewrite
- If hook feels predictable → rewrite

Only output hooks that feel sharp, uncomfortable, and new.
